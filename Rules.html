Our Library Rules

  1. Return books on time.

  2. Maintain silence inside the library.

  3. Do not damage books.

  4. Keep food and drinks away from books.

  5. Respect library staff and other members.

  6. Keep the library clean and tidy.

Following these rules helps everyone enjoy a better reading experience.
